About the Data & Transparency

Data Sources

Compiled from m-mahadi/top5-football-dataset, integrating match feeds and tracking statistics from FBref, Understat, SofaScore, and game attributes from EA FC / SoFIFA.

Honest Missing Data Policy

When a stat was not tracked for a match or player, it is displayed as N/A. Missing values are never silently converted to zero in averages, comparison bars, or similarity vectors.

EA FC Game Estimates

Attributes such as Overall Rating and Transfer Value are video game estimates from EA FC / SoFIFA, clearly labelled as game ratings rather than official transfer valuations.

Minutes Reliability Filter

Per-90 metrics derived from very low sample sizes are statistically volatile. Players with fewer than 5 full 90-minute matches (450 minutes) are flagged with a warning badge and filtered by default.
