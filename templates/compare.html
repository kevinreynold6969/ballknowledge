Why Relative Bar Scaling?

Fixed arbitrary maximums (e.g. 20 goals) distort comparisons across positions. By scaling each bar relative to the leader among the currently compared players, Ball Knowledge provides an immediate visual representation of who leads each metric and by what relative margin.
